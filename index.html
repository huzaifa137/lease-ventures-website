Who We Are

YOUR TRUSTED AUTOMOTIVE PARTNER

Lease Ventures Uganda Limited is a Ugandan-owned private limited company, registered with URSB and listed with the Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Assets (PPDA) Authority.

We bring everything an owner needs under one roof — from the vehicle or motorcycle itself to servicing, genuine parts, clearing and training — so you can focus on moving.

  • Quality automotive solutions, delivered to your utmost satisfaction
  • Toyota, Honda, Yamaha and Lifan — built for our roads and terrain
  • Professional technicians and dedicated expert instructors
