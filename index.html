The Lease Ventures Edge

Lease Ventures Uganda Limited is a Ugandan-owned private limited company. Our vision is to be the most trusted automotive partner, turning automotive ownership into a joyful experience.

From the showroom to the garage and the border, one team looks after your vehicle and your fleet.

  • Ugandan-owned, URSB-registered and PPDA-listed
  • Toyota vehicles and Honda, Yamaha and Lifan motorcycles
  • Professional installation by experienced mechanics
  • Driver and rider training as part of after-sales care
