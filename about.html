Who We Are

Lease Ventures Uganda Limited is a Ugandan-owned private limited company registered under URSB No. 80020002067978 and listed on the Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Assets (PPDA) Authority under No. PRV/SPLS/26194/SEP/19.

We sell and lease Toyota vehicles and Honda, Yamaha and Lifan motorcycles, supply genuine parts and accessories, run a full-service garage, train drivers and riders, and handle clearing and forwarding.

  • Quality automotive solutions to the utmost satisfaction of our customers
  • Vehicles designed for our rough roads and terrain
  • Based in Bukoto Ntinda Road, Kampala
