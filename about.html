Our Story

LEASE VENTURES UGANDA LIMITED

Lease Ventures Uganda Limited is a Ugandan-owned private limited company registered under the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB) and listed with the Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Assets (PPDA) Authority.

We offer a complete automotive experience: Toyota vehicles, Honda, Yamaha and Lifan motorcycles, a premium garage, genuine spare parts and accessories, clearing & forwarding, and defensive driver and rider training — all designed around one goal: keeping you moving.
