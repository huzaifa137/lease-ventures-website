After-Sales Training

SAFER DRIVERS, LONGER-LASTING MACHINES

Lease Ventures Uganda Ltd offers driver’s/riders’ training to its clients on a case-by-case basis, as part of its after-sales services for motor vehicles and motorcycles within the warranty period.

This is done to impart the skills drivers and riders need to ensure longevity and consistent performance of the machine, and cost effectiveness in terms of fuel usage, repairs and servicing.

The company has dedicated expert instructors who leave nothing to chance to develop a safer and more confident driver or rider with good observational skills — able to recognise and react to any hazard that may present itself on the road.

What You Gain

SKILLS THAT PAY OFF

Training outcomes that protect people, vehicles and budgets.

Observation

Good observational skills that spot hazards early.

Reaction

Confident, correct responses to hazards on the road.

Cost Effectiveness

Better fuel usage and fewer repairs and servicing costs.

Consistent Performance

Skills that protect the machine and extend its life.
